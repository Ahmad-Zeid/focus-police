Focus Police: session runner

Leave this on the second screen. It shows what to do now and how long is left. It makes no sound: when the time is up the whole screen turns red and stays red until you switch. To switch: stop, clap 3×, then click the big button (or press Space with this window focused). The click is the timestamp. Read README.md first.

SESSION
S1…S6Training sessions. Take the watch off and put it back on between sessions, and change something small each time (seat, cup, where the phone lies).
TESTSame format, but kept away from training. The final score comes from this one only.
NATURALNo script: real work, then real phone use, three times. Tests how the model does on normal behaviour.
OTHERHANDPhone in the hand without the watch. Test only: measures a known weakness.
TIMING

Every activity comes up once per round, in a new shuffled order each round. Several short blocks teach the model more than one long block, because each block is a fresh start with the hand placed slightly differently. The target is a minimum, not a cut-off: the label ends when you click. PILOT is 2 activities × 20 s. NATURAL has its own fixed timing.
